# WonderLabs Identity

## Who We Are

WonderLabs builds experiences that families remember.

We believe technology should create memories, not consume attention.

We do not chase features.

We design moments.

---

## Our Mission

Help families create meaningful moments together through magical digital experiences that naturally lead back into the real world.

---

## Our Beliefs

Technology should disappear.

Wonder comes first.

Emotion beats features.

Children are never rushed.

Parents are participants, not observers.

Simple beats clever.

Offline memories are more valuable than online engagement.

Every interaction should strengthen family connection.

---

## Our Promise

Every product we build must leave families with something they can remember after the screen is turned off.

---

## What We Refuse

We refuse to design addictive experiences.

We refuse meaningless notifications.

We refuse engagement for the sake of engagement.

We refuse complexity that does not create wonder.

---

## Our North Star

Technology fades.

Wonder remains.
