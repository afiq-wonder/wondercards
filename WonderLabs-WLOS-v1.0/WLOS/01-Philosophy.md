# 01 — WonderLabs Philosophy

## Mission
Strengthen family relationships through meaningful shared experiences.

## Vision
Build the world's most trusted creative production company for families.

## Core belief
Technology should disappear. Relationships should remain.

## Founding principles
- Systems over heroes.
- Standards before speed.
- Measure the process, support the person.
- Every product improves WonderOS.
- Every pipeline improves WonderLabs.
- Offline-first family experience.
- Memory before screen time.
- Quality is measured, not assumed.
- Trust is earned through consistency.
