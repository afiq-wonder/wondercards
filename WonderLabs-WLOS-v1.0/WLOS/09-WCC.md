# 09 — Wonder Command Center (WCC)

WCC is the executive and operational monitoring layer.

The CEO should understand company health in under 60 seconds.

Required signals:
- Company health
- Pipeline health
- Active production
- WQG pass rate
- Average WCS
- PRL distribution
- Repair queue
- Release queue
- System autonomy
- CEO intervention rate
- Family outcomes
- Critical risks

WCC should surface exceptions, trends, and decisions requiring attention.
