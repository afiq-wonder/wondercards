# 10 — Company Metrics

## Quality
WQG pass rate, average WCS, critical failure rate, repair success rate, post-release defect rate.

## Production
Pipeline velocity, cycle time, repair time, publish velocity, automation rate, rework rate.

## Product
Completion rate, replay rate, parent participation, family satisfaction, retention, Wonder Moments created.

## Company
System Autonomy Index, CEO Intervention Rate, builder satisfaction, pipeline ROI, factory efficiency, decision latency.

Metrics must drive improvement, not vanity.
