# 14 — CEO Doctrine

> The CEO does not build every product.  
> The CEO builds the system that builds every product.

The CEO defines vision, protects the mission, sets standards, allocates resources, removes systemic bottlenecks, improves the factory, monitors through exceptions and trusted metrics, and intervenes only where the system cannot yet decide safely.

The objective is not to make the CEO absent.

The objective is to make the company capable.
