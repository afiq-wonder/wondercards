# 15 — Architecture Principles

- WLOS alignment before implementation.
- Explicit contracts over hidden assumptions.
- Modular pipelines over monoliths.
- Deterministic validation before probabilistic evaluation.
- Human-governed AI.
- Offline-first customer experience.
- Traceability by default.
- Version everything that can change outcomes.
- Fail visibly and safely.
- Design for repair.
- Prefer reusable capabilities over product-specific shortcuts.
