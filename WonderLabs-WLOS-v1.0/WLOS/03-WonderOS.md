# 03 — WonderOS

WonderOS is WonderLabs' core creative operating system and primary intellectual property.

## Responsibilities
- Orchestrate production workflows.
- Enforce schemas and standards.
- Route outputs through evaluation and repair.
- Calculate quality and confidence metrics.
- Track production readiness.
- Collect operational data.
- Package and release approved outputs.
- Surface exceptions to the correct owner.

WonderOS does not replace human vision, family empathy, governance, or accountability.

Its design objective is to reduce repeated manual judgement while preserving explicit human control over mission, standards, policy, and exceptions.
