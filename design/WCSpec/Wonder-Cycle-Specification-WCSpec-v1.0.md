**Status:** Draft v1.0\
**Depends On:** WLOS v1.0, WKS v1.0\
**Authority:** Universal Operating Model\
**Owner:** WonderLabs

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Foreword

WonderLabs believes that everything meaningful grows through cycles.

Families grow. Products grow. Builders grow. Pipelines grow. The company
grows.

Growth is continuous.

Wonder Cycle is the universal model that explains how everything inside
WonderLabs evolves.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Chapter 1 --- First Principle

> Everything in WonderLabs exists inside a Wonder Cycle.

Nothing is static. Nothing is ever truly finished. Everything is always
becoming better.

# Chapter 3 --- Seven States

## Identity

Current state of a family, product, builder, pipeline or company.

## Potential

The next opportunity that can be explored.

## Experience

An action or event that produces evidence.

## Reflection

Turning experience into learning.

## Growth

Measurable improvement.

## Evolution

Updating the system based on learning.

## Identity

A stronger identity begins the next cycle.

# Chapter 11 --- Wonder Cycle Rules

Every Wonder Cycle must include:

-   Identity
-   Trigger
-   Experience
-   Reflection
-   Measurement
-   Evolution
-   Repeatability

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Chapter 12 --- Metrics

Each cycle should produce measurable outputs.

Examples:

Family: - Wonder Moments - Growth Traits

Product: - WCS - WQG - Retention

Pipeline: - Cycle Time - Automation Rate

Builder: - Knowledge Growth - Contribution

Company: - Trust - System Autonomy - CEO Intervention Rate
