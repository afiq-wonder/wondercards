# 18 — AI Governance

AI supports production. AI does not own WonderLabs' mission or accountability.

- Human owners remain accountable.
- AI outputs require policy-based evaluation.
- Prompts, models, schemas, and evaluators must be versioned.
- Sensitive data must not be used without explicit governance.
- Child safety failures are critical failures.
- Model changes require regression evaluation.
- Release-affecting AI decisions must be explainable.
- Customer experiences remain offline-first unless explicitly approved.
