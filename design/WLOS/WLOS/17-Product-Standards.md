# 17 — Product Standards

Every WonderLabs product should:
- Strengthen connection between family members.
- Respect the child's developmental stage.
- Encourage participation, imagination, and conversation.
- Avoid unnecessary screen dependence.
- Produce a meaningful offline action or memory where appropriate.
- Feel recognisably WonderLabs.
- Be safe, accessible, understandable, and trustworthy.
- Include measurable intended outcomes.
