# 07 — Wonder Confidence Score (WCS)

| Score | Decision |
|---:|---|
| 95–100 | Publish |
| 91–94 | Testing pool |
| 85–90 | Repair |
| Below 85 | Reject or redesign |

A high composite score cannot override a failed critical safety, governance, legal, or brand gate.

WCS must be explainable, reproducible, versioned, calibrated, and auditable.
