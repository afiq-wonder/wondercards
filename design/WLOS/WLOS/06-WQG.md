# 06 — Wonder Quality Gate (WQG)

WQG is the mandatory multi-stage quality control system.

## Evaluation dimensions
- Safety
- Age appropriateness
- Language clarity
- Emotional quality
- Educational value
- Parent participation
- Offline engagement
- Wonder Voice
- Character consistency
- World consistency
- Accessibility
- Production completeness

A product may not progress when a critical safety, policy, identity, or structural gate fails.

Every score must include evidence, reason codes, and repair guidance.
