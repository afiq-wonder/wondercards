# 02 — Company Operating Model

```text
CEO
 ↓
Wonder Command Center
 ↓
WonderOS
 ↓
WonderStudio
 ↓
Builders
 ↓
Products
 ↓
Families
 ↓
Insights
 ↺ Improve WonderOS
```

The CEO owns vision, standards, strategic direction, capital allocation, culture, and system improvement.

WonderOS coordinates production rules, workflows, quality enforcement, readiness, metrics, and release decisions.

WonderStudio is the production environment in which approved pipelines generate, evaluate, repair, package, and publish outputs.

Builders improve pipelines and systems. Builders do not bypass the system to create hidden dependencies.

Families are the final beneficiaries. Their trust, safety, joy, participation, and memories are the ultimate outcomes.
