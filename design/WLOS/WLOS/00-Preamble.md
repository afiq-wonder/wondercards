# 00 — Preamble

WonderLabs exists to strengthen family relationships through meaningful shared experiences.

We are not building a company dependent on heroic effort, constant founder approval, or undocumented judgement. We are building a company whose systems can repeatedly produce trusted products without losing its identity.

Products may change. Technology may change. AI models may change. The operating principles of WonderLabs must remain clear, measurable, and governable.

WLOS is a living constitution. It defines how WonderLabs thinks, decides, builds, measures, improves, and protects its mission.
